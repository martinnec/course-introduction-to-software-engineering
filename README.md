This is a sample repository for Introduction to Software Engineering (NSWI041) course at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University.

ExploreLocal connects travelers (including families) with local guides for unique tours and activities. We help travelers find great local guides and avoid tourist traps, and we help guides grow their businesses. Travelers easily discover authentic and enriching experiences, securely book, and connect directly with top local guides. Guides gain increased visibility and streamlined booking management.

See the [Wiki](https://github.com/martinnec/course-introduction-to-software-engineering/wiki) for more details.
